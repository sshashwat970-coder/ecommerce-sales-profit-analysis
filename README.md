E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset to understand sales, profit, customer, product, and regional performance.

The project uses **Microsoft Excel** for data cleaning and exploratory analysis and **Power BI** for interactive visualization and dashboard development.

## 2. Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Compare sales and profit across categories and regions
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends
- Identify the top 10 products by sales
- Identify the top 10 customers by sales
- Build an interactive business dashboard

## 3. Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Pivot Tables
- Power BI
- Data Cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## 4. Dataset

The project uses the **Sample Superstore** dataset containing **9,994 sales records**.

The dataset includes information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Categories and Sub-Categories
- Regions
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discounts
- Profit

A `Profit_Margin` column was also created during the data preparation stage.

## 5. Data Analysis

The following analyses were performed in Excel:

1. Sales & Profit by Category
2. Sales & Profit by Region
3. Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
4. Top 10 Products by Sales
5. Top 10 Customers by Sales

## 6. Power BI Dashboard

The final dashboard includes:

### KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers

### Visualizations
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
- Sales & Profit by Category
- Sales & Profit by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Customers by Sales

### Filters
- Region
- Category
- Customer Segment

## 7. Limitations

- The project uses a historical Sample Superstore dataset.
- The analysis is descriptive and exploratory.
- No predictive modeling or forecasting was performed.
